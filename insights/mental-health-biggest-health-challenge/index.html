Mental health is now perceived as the biggest health challenge of our time.

45% of adults across 30 countries identify mental health issues as one of the main health challenges in their societies today, ahead of cancer, obesity and cardiovascular disease.

This reinforces something we strongly believe at Cozecare: mental healthcare cannot remain something people only turn to when they are already struggling.

It needs to become more accessible, continuous and proactive.

That’s what we’re building at Cozecare, mental health support that goes beyond isolated moments of care and becomes part of everyday life.

Source: Ipsos Health Service Report 2025. 23,172 adults online (under age 75) surveyed in 30 countries between July and August 2025.
