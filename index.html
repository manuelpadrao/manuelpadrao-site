Mission

Technology should bring people closer, not push them further apart.

I believe the best technology solves meaningful human problems. My work is focused on building products and companies that improve how people connect, support one another and access care.

My Story

Building something that matters.

My journey as a founder started with a simple observation: some of the most important challenges people face are still solved with fragmented systems, outdated processes and unnecessary complexity.

That led me to build CozeCare — with the ambition of using technology to create a better, simpler and more human experience around care.

Today, my focus is on growing CozeCare, learning from the people we serve and building a company capable of creating meaningful long-term impact.
