Manuel Padrão is the Founder & CEO of Cozecare, the largest mental health platform in Portugal. He built it around two problems he lived firsthand.

1
Finding the right psychologist.

Without a good match, patients drop out. Cozecare helps people find a psychologist online who fits.

2
Support between sessions.

Without support, patients feel alone and drop out. Cozecare keeps them supported every day.

Today: 300,000+ people covered by 300+ psychologists, in multiple languages, through online therapy and online psychology.
